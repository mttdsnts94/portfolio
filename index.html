banking

Concept Project for Unicredit app

This concept project explores an adaptive approach to banking, designed to support people through different life stages and evolving goals. Starting from the idea that money is not just an individual matter but also something shared within relationships, I crafted an experience that adapts both to personal needs and to those of the people users share their lives with.

The screens were designed based on these pillars, with a strong focus on shifting interactions from a medium-term perspective to a hyper short-term one—favoring frequent, fast, and rewarding sessions that make the banking experience more dynamic, relevant, and seamlessly integrated into everyday life.

Inactive app

Unique access

The app welcomes both the client who already has a bank account and the prospect who wants to start their experience or access to UniCredit products (e.g. Opening a bank account).

Active app

Your today’s budget

For general users, the main purpose of the app is to facilitate a maniacal control over the single daily expenses. This becomes seamless through the connections with other accounts and cards along with the increase of cash free transactions.

client

My Bank area

Customers can flow easily from the daily budget to the more core banking areas. How do you prefer to manage your account? Which are your most frequent actions? Are you going abroad? In your personal area you can have an overview of your finances, set up buckets, divide your accounts, monitor your investments etc.

Active app

Fast and contextual services

The app enables one-click services that can even be triggered by proximity (e.g. to a branch, or to a shop). Fast features are different for prospects and clients.

clientprospect

Your profile, your circles

The profile page allows you to have an overview of your personal data, but also to manage your profile settings (e.g. Investment style, saving style), your communities, subscriptions and privacy consents in a ‘community like’ setting.
